Digital Intake Forms Rollout

A staged implementation guide for replacing paper intake forms with secured tablets and role-specific apps—designed around the people adopting the workflow, not just the software.

AudienceOwners, managers, front desk, and service providers
PurposeLaunch securely with fewer support tickets
Format~40-page tabbed guide with FAQs and appendices

Lead with why, then teach how

The guide begins with the security and client-experience reasons for the change. Device management restricts unrelated use, helps protect personally identifiable information, and keeps guest data off personal devices. Digital intake also removes scanning, shredding, and paper storage from the process.

  • Providers review forms and sign off through the team app.
  • Guided service notes produce consistent records for reporting.
  • Guests complete forms on location without front-desk re-entry.

A staged rollout

01ReviewRead the guide at least three weeks before automation.
02Prepare devicesPurchase, configure, and install secured tablets.
03LearnPractice form operations and the team app.
04TrainTeach each role one to two weeks before launch.
05TestSend test forms and role-play the guest experience.
06VerifyComplete final checks around go-live.
Two rollout dates create a safe runwayThe dashboard activates about two weeks before automations so teams can explore, test, and become familiar before the new workflow becomes live.

Make support easier to give

Vendor supportBugs where documented behavior and actual behavior do not match.
Support CenterTraining, feedback, guidance, and workflow questions.
Every ticketInclude the profile, location, system, steps, expected result, timing, recurrence, and full-screen evidence.

The guide also reminds readers that the person responding is there to help—a small human detail that improves the support partnership.

Enablement principle

Successful adoption comes from explaining the reason, preparing each role, creating time to practice, and teaching people how to ask for useful support.

What to notice

  • Security and client experience come before button-click instructions.
  • The rollout separates preparation, learning, training, testing, and launch.
  • Role-play lets staff experience the workflow as a guest would.
  • Support routing and ticket quality are treated as part of enablement.
